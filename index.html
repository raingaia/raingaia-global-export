Real claims, not marketing copy

Three concrete things you can verify yourself, not just take our word for.

A

Source-relative, not one-size-fits-all

Every source — a host, a sensor point, a physiological channel — gets its own learned baseline. Nothing is scored against a single global "normal."

B

Published, not proprietary

Raingaia Seismos's stiffness-loss estimates are grounded in published structural engineering research (Worden et al. 2012; Salawu 1997) — methods you can look up and evaluate independently, not a black-box model.

C

Isolated, not shared

Each Raingaia Network customer is provisioned their own analysis engine and database. Traffic and findings are never pooled into a shared multi-tenant store.

02

The shared discipline

The same four principles show up in every RAINGAIA product, adapted to what each domain's sensors actually produce.

01
Source-relative baseline
Every source gets its own learned baseline instead of one global "normal." A warmup period establishes what normal looks like specifically for that source before anything is scored against it.
02
Multi-channel evidence fusion
Independent signal channels are tracked and decayed separately, then fused into one composite assessment — cross-checking multiple views of the same event instead of trusting a single signal alone.
03
Threshold-free, probabilistic decisions
Verdicts come from a source-relative probability distribution over possible states, not a fixed magic-number threshold — and the same input always produces the same output.
04
Tamper-evident audit trail
Every decision is written to a cryptographically chained audit log — each entry linked to the one before it, so a retroactively altered record breaks the chain.
