About

A small team building evidence-grade software

RAINGAIA is a Florida-based software company. We build monitoring software for domains where a wrong or unexplainable verdict has real consequences — a network security finding someone has to act on, a structural inspection priority someone has to schedule around, a physiological signal someone has to trust. Across all three, we've made the same bet: that a system built on a source-relative learned baseline, multi-channel evidence fusion, and a tamper-evident audit trail produces better and more defensible decisions than a system built on fixed thresholds and a black-box score.

What we build

Three independent products, each complete on its own: Raingaia Network for network traffic anomaly detection, Raingaia Seismos for vibration-based structural health monitoring, and Raingaia Bio for hardware-independent bioelectric signal analysis. See our technical approach for how they share a reasoning discipline without sharing a codebase.

Where we are

We are a small, early-stage team. Raingaia Network is our most active product commercially today; Seismos and Bio are built on the same discipline for their respective domains. We'd rather say that plainly than overstate our size — if you're evaluating us, ask us directly about scale, deployment history, or anything else you need to know.
